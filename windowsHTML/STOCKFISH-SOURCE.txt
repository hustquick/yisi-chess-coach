Stockfish browser engine source and build information
=====================================================

Bundled engine: Stockfish.js 18.0.8 lite single-thread, based on Stockfish 18
Stockfish.js source: https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js
Stockfish source: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
Stockfish 18 tag: sf_18
Stockfish commit: cb3d4ee9b47d0c5aae855b12379378ea1439675c
License: GNU GPL v3 (see STOCKFISH-GPL-3.0.txt)

The single-thread WebAssembly build works without SharedArrayBuffer or
cross-origin isolation. For direct file:// use, engine-data.js stores the
WebAssembly bytes as Base64. The application reconstructs the upstream
Stockfish factory inside a local Blob Worker and performs all analysis on the
device. No chess position is uploaded to a server.

The unmodified Stockfish 18 source used by the other platforms in this
repository is available at ..\shared\Stockfish. The exact Stockfish.js
port source is available from the project URL above.

chess.js source: https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js
Bundled chess.js version: 1.4.0
